GAMEPLAY MECHANICS & TROLL TRAPS

Every single level is 100% mathematically and physically beatable! While the game is designed to look unfairly cruel, all 200 trials are handcrafted and strictly verified for solvability. The physics engine enforces precise jump velocities, clear landing platforms, safe spawn zones, and crawl duct headroom. If you keep dying, observe trap cadences, use your crouch/crawl stance, or adjust your leap timing.

Crawling is a core tactical mechanic! Hold S or ▼ (Down Arrow) to drop your devil onto all fours. While holding down, press D or ▶ (Right Arrow) to crawl forward.

  • Hitbox Reduction: Your vertical collision height drops from 24px to 12px.
  • Dodging Razors: Head-height spinning sawblades will decapitate a standing player, but you can crawl right beneath them unharmed!
  • Claustrophobic Ducts: Certain levels feature tight 35px tunnels that can only be traversed on all fours.

That is the signature Adaptive Shifting Door! In classic rage platformer tradition, certain levels feature evasive exit doors that startle and hop away when approached. However, our physics engine performs obstacle-aware AABB clearance checks against all level solids, walls, and ceilings before every hop. The door will NEVER jump into a wall, become embedded in the ceiling, or softlock the game. Once its evasion hops are exhausted (or if no clear alternative space exists), the door settles permanently, ready for you to step inside!

Rolling boulders are fully simulated physics entities (RollingBall class). When triggered by proximity, they roll downhill with gravitational momentum, rotational angular velocity, and kinetic rebound. When a boulder slams into a solid wall, it shatters violently into explosive rock debris particles. You can jump over boulders if timed right, or bait them to smash into walls to clear narrow pathways.

Devil's Trial features exactly 200 Handcrafted Progressive Levels divided across 10 distinct architectural chapters:

  • Levels 1 to 20: The Descent of Deception & Whispering Caverns (Fundamental troll mechanics, falling floors, and rolling boulders).

  • Levels 101 to 199: The Mind Fracture & Infernal Gauntlet (Reversed control zones, dual boulder chases, and high-altitude precision leaps).
  • Level 200: "The Devil's Ultimate Reckoning" (The Grand Finale masterpiece with 16 alive hazards and a celebratory victory cinematic).

All 200 trials are 100% handcrafted and deterministic. Trap layouts, platform placements, and crusher timings are consistent on every run, enabling speedrunners and dedicated players to master muscle memory, develop reliable routes, and conquer all 200 trials through pure skill.

SAVES & DEVICE STORAGE

Devil's Trial features an automatic instant-save engine. Every time you clear a level, incur a death, or change your theme, your progress is immediately serialized into HTML5 localStorage under the key devils_trial_save_v1. You never need to manually save your game.

Because the game uses HTML5 LocalStorage, your save file resides in your browser's local sandbox profile directory:

  • Windows (Chrome): %LocalAppData%\Google\Chrome\User Data\Default\Local Storage\leveldb\
  • Windows (Edge): %LocalAppData%\Microsoft\Edge\User Data\Default\Local Storage\leveldb\
  • Windows (Brave): %LocalAppData%\BraveSoftware\Brave-Browser\User Data\Default\Local Storage\leveldb\
  • Windows (Firefox): %APPDATA%\Mozilla\Firefox\Profiles\<Profile>\storage\default\
  • macOS (Safari): ~/Library/Containers/com.apple.Safari/Data/Library/LocalStorage/
  • macOS (Chrome): ~/Library/Application Support/Google/Chrome/Default/Local Storage/leveldb/

Tip: Click the DATA button in the game header to view your exact on-disk path or open it with 1-click in File Explorer!

Yes, 100%! Under modern W3C Progressive Web App standards, a PWA shares the identical browser storage partition with normal browser tabs under the same origin. All your unlocked levels, death counts, and themes persist seamlessly between browser tabs and the installed PWA desktop window.

Click the DATA button in the top-right header, then click EXPORT SAVE JSON. A modal will appear displaying your live save payload with two easy options: "COPY JSON" to copy to clipboard, or "DOWNLOAD .JSON" to save a timestamped backup file to your downloads folder.

TECHNICAL, PWA & OFFLINE CAPABILITIES

Yes! Thanks to our Progressive Web App (PWA) Service Worker (sw.js), all game scripts, audio synthesizers, physics calculations, and themes are precached in your browser's Cache Storage. Once loaded once, you can disconnect Wi-Fi or turn on Airplane Mode, and the game will launch and run flawlessly offline.

Devil's Trial is heavily optimized and runs at 60 FPS on almost all modern hardware. If you experience frame drops:

  • Ensure Hardware Acceleration is turned ON in your browser settings (Settings ➔ System ➔ Use graphics acceleration when available).
  • Close background browser tabs that may be consuming high CPU/GPU resources.
  • Check that your monitor refresh rate is set to 60Hz or your browser is not frame-capped by power-saver mode.
