🎮 GAMEPLAY MECHANICS & TROLL TRAPS

Every single level is 100% mathematically beatable! While the game is designed to look unfairly cruel, it is backed by an algorithmic Semantic Anti-Checkmate Guarantee. The trap placement engine checks mutual exclusion rules: for example, a rolling boulder will never spawn at the exact same location as an unavoidable spike, and ceiling crushers will never drop without a rhythmically timed passage window. If you keep dying, observe the visual telegraphs, use your crawl stance, or alter your approach speed.

Crawling is a core tactical mechanic! Hold S or ▼ (Down Arrow) to drop your devil onto all fours. While holding down, press D or ▶ (Right Arrow) to crawl forward.

  • Hitbox Reduction: Your vertical collision height drops from 32px to 16px.
  • Dodging Razors: Head-height spinning sawblades will decapitate a standing player, but you can crawl right beneath them unharmed!
  • Claustrophobic Ducts: Certain levels feature tight 24px tunnels that can only be traversed by crawling.

That is the Troll Shifting Door! In classic troll platformer fashion, certain levels feature deceptive victory portals. If you rush blindly at full speed, proximity sensors trigger the door to teleport across the room, slam shut, or drop into a subterranean cavern. To beat shifting doors, inspect the terrain for secondary secret alcoves, wait out their animation, or trigger them safely from an elevated vantage point.

Rolling boulders are fully simulated physics entities (RollingBall class). When triggered by proximity, they roll downhill with gravitational momentum, rotational angular velocity, and kinetic rebound. When a boulder slams into a solid wall, it shatters violently into explosive rock debris particles. You can jump over boulders if timed right, or bait them to smash into walls to clear narrow pathways.

Devil's Trial features a hybrid progression architecture:

  • Levels 1 to 4: Handcrafted, bespoke gauntlets designed to teach and test core mechanics (Descent, Squeeze, Pit of Deceit, Crusher Alley).
  • Levels 5 to 1000+: Powered by the parametric Endless AI Level Generator (AILevelGenerator). It algorithmically constructs infinite varied levels across 8 distinct architectural topologies (Crawl Ducts, Spire Climbs, Boulder Ramps, Twin Routes, etc.) with progressive difficulty scaling.

Chaos Mode introduces high-entropy dynamic shuffling. Instead of static, predictable hazards, trap types swap between candidate positions on every death. Spikes may transform into spinning sawblades, floor tiles may collapse without warning, and piston crushers might alter their stroke cadence. You can toggle between Chaos/Adaptive Mode and Static Mode via the AI Director badge in the top header.

🧠 THE ADAPTIVE AI DIRECTOR

The AI Director runs a lightweight client-side reinforcement learning engine (DevilAI). Every frame, it tracks your kinematics:

  • Horizontal Velocity & Rush Distance: How fast and aggressively you sprint forward without stopping.
  • Hesitation / Idle Time: How long you stand still contemplating obstacle timing.
  • Jump Frequency: How frequently you bunny-hop across platforms.
  • Death Coordinates & Fatal Hazard: Exactly where and how you perished.

It then classifies you into one of 4 profiles: RUSHER, CAUTIOUS, HOPPER, or BALANCED, and deploys custom counter-traps tailored to punish your specific habits!

💾 SAVES & DEVICE STORAGE

Devil's Trial features an automatic instant-save engine. Every time you clear a level, incur a death, change your theme, or toggle AI modes, your progress is immediately serialized into HTML5 localStorage under the key devils_trial_save_v1. You never need to manually save your game.

Because the game uses HTML5 LocalStorage, your save file resides in your browser's local sandbox profile directory:

  • Windows (Chrome): %LocalAppData%\Google\Chrome\User Data\Default\Local Storage\leveldb\
  • Windows (Edge): %LocalAppData%\Microsoft\Edge\User Data\Default\Local Storage\leveldb\
  • Windows (Brave): %LocalAppData%\BraveSoftware\Brave-Browser\User Data\Default\Local Storage\leveldb\
  • Windows (Firefox): %APPDATA%\Mozilla\Firefox\Profiles\<Profile>\storage\default\
  • macOS (Safari): ~/Library/Containers/com.apple.Safari/Data/Library/LocalStorage/
  • macOS (Chrome): ~/Library/Application Support/Google/Chrome/Default/Local Storage/leveldb/

💡 Tip: Click the 💾 DATA button in the game header to view your exact on-disk path or open it with 1-click in File Explorer!

Yes, 100%! Under modern W3C Progressive Web App standards, a PWA shares the identical browser storage partition with normal browser tabs under the same origin. All your unlocked levels, death counts, themes, and AI telemetry persist seamlessly between browser tabs and the installed PWA desktop window.

Click the 💾 DATA button in the top-right header, then click 📄 EXPORT SAVE JSON. A modal will appear displaying your live save payload with two easy options: "📋 COPY JSON" to copy to clipboard, or "💾 DOWNLOAD .JSON" to save a timestamped backup file to your downloads folder.

Click 💾 DATA in the header, scroll down to the red ⚠️ PERMANENT DATA DESTRUCTION section, and click 🔥 PERMANENTLY DESTROY ALL DATA & RESET. This invokes a hard reset, permanently removes the storage key, and returns you to Level 1 with 0 deaths and freshly cleared AI weights.

Devil's Trial is heavily optimized and runs at 60 FPS on almost all modern hardware. If you experience frame drops:

  • Ensure Hardware Acceleration is turned ON in your browser settings (Settings ➔ System ➔ Use graphics acceleration when available).
  • Close background browser tabs that may be consuming high CPU/GPU resources.
  • Check that your monitor refresh rate is set to 60Hz or your browser is not frame-capped by power-saver mode.
