AI Director & Anti-Checkmate Guarantee

Our client-side Machine Learning model analyzes your movement dynamics in real time to provide fair, personalized platforming challenge.

• RUSHER: High horizontal speed, sprints blindly ➔ countered with delayed drop spikes & roll boulders.
• CAUTIOUS: Hesitates before jumping ➔ countered with rhythmic piston crushers.
• HOPPER: Bunny-hops constantly ➔ countered with ceiling-mounted sawblades.
• BALANCED: Mixed tactical player ➔ countered with hybrid trap selections.

Traps pass through a strict compatibility algorithm: rolling boulders cannot share elevation corridors with floor collapses in evasion zones, and dropping spikes never sandwich players with floor spikes. Every obstacle is solvable!
